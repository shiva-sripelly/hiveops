# Q3 2026 Business Review

## Headline results
Northwind closed Q3 2026 with total revenue of $4.82 million, up 18% from $4.08 million in Q2. Net revenue retention reached 112%, and gross margin improved to 78%. Monthly logo churn fell to 2.1% from 2.6% in Q2, driven by the new onboarding program.

## Regional performance
EMEA was the fastest-growing region, with revenue up 27% quarter over quarter after the launch of EU data residency. North America remains the largest region by revenue. APAC grew 12%, while LATAM was flat and missed its target by 9% because two large deals slipped into Q4.

## Product highlights
The Growth plan became the best-selling plan by new bookings. Churn prediction is now used by 64% of Growth and Enterprise customers. The Snowflake connector launched in August and was adopted by 140 customers within six weeks.

## Risks
The main risks for Q4 are LATAM pipeline coverage, a rising cost of cloud infrastructure (up 11% in Q3), and hiring delays in the customer success team. The Acme Cloud contract renewal in Q1 2027 is a cost risk if usage keeps growing at the current rate.

## Q4 priorities
1. Recover the LATAM pipeline with a dedicated partner program.
2. Launch the Enterprise audit log feature by November 15.
3. Reduce infrastructure cost per customer by 8% through reserved capacity.
4. Hire 6 customer success managers, prioritising EMEA.

The quarterly business review was prepared by Daniel Okafor, VP Finance, and reviewed by the executive team on October 6, 2026.
