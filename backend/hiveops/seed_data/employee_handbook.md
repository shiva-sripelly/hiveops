# Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook

## Remote work policy
Northwind is remote-first. Employees may work from any country where Northwind has a legal entity: the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany and India. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year requires written approval from People Operations. Core collaboration hours are 15:00 to 18:00 UTC, and every team keeps at least three hours of overlap each working day.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per year, plus local public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by March 31. Requests longer than 10 consecutive working days need manager approval at least 4 weeks in advance. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for all parents.

## Expense policy
Expenses must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days, with an itemised receipt. Expenses up to $500 are approved by the employee's manager. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 also need approval from the department head. Anything above $5,000 requires sign-off from the Finance Director, Priya Raman, before the purchase is made. Alcohol, first-class travel and personal entertainment are not reimbursable.

## Travel policy
Book all travel through the Navan travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. The hotel limit is $250 per night in most cities and $350 per night in London, New York, San Francisco and Zurich. Daily meal allowance is $75.

## Information security
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company account. Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and receive security updates within 7 days of release. Customer data is classified as Confidential and must never be stored on personal devices or shared in public Slack channels. Report suspected security incidents to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Learning and development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences. Certifications relevant to the role are reimbursed in full when approved by the manager in advance.
