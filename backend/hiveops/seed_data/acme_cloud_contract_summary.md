# Acme Cloud Master Services Agreement Summary

## Parties and term
The master services agreement is between Northwind Analytics Ltd. and Acme Cloud Inc. for managed cloud hosting. The initial term is 24 months, starting February 1, 2025 and ending January 31, 2027.

## Fees
The committed annual fee is $120,000, billed quarterly in advance. Usage above the committed capacity is billed at $0.09 per compute-hour. Fees may increase by up to 5% at renewal.

## Renewal and termination
The agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term. To avoid automatic renewal, Northwind must send notice by December 2, 2026. Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days to cure.

## Service levels
Acme Cloud commits to 99.95% monthly availability. Service credits are 5% of the monthly fee for availability below 99.95% and 15% below 99.5%, capped at 30% of the monthly fee.

## Liability and data protection
Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or data protection obligations. Acme Cloud must notify Northwind of any personal data breach within 48 hours and must support customer data deletion within 30 days of termination.

## Key risks
The automatic renewal date is close, usage is growing faster than the committed capacity, and the 5% renewal uplift could add $6,000 per year. The legal owner of this contract is Maria Gonzalez, General Counsel.
