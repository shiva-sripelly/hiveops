# Northwind Insights Product FAQ

## What is Northwind Insights?
Northwind Insights is a customer analytics platform that unifies product usage, billing and CRM data into dashboards and churn predictions. It is sold as a monthly or annual subscription.

## Pricing plans
The Starter plan costs $49 per month and includes 3 seats and 1 data source. The Growth plan costs $199 per month and includes 15 seats, 10 data sources and churn prediction. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds SSO, audit logs, a dedicated success manager and unlimited data sources. Annual billing gives a 15% discount on Starter and Growth.

## Service level agreement
Enterprise customers receive a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If uptime falls below 99.9%, customers receive a 10% service credit; below 99.0% the credit is 25%. Planned maintenance is announced 72 hours in advance and does not count as downtime.

## Data retention and security
Raw event data is retained for 13 months on Starter and Growth, and for up to 7 years on Enterprise. All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Northwind Insights is SOC 2 Type II certified, and customers can choose EU or US data residency.

## Integrations
Native connectors are available for Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, Snowflake, BigQuery and Segment. A REST API and webhooks are available on every plan. Custom connectors can be built by the professional services team for Enterprise customers.

## Support
Starter customers get email support with a 2 business day response time. Growth customers get chat and email support with an 8 hour response time. Enterprise customers get 24/7 support with a 1 hour response time for critical incidents.
